# Automated Recaps

Please continue to create your own notes to track the things you think are important or that your character would think are important. The automated recap is based on a diarize script transcribing our game (and it doesn't always get things perfect) and then Claude deciding what would make the best narrative, not necessarily what would be most important to remember. I'm just providing this as a courtesy based on how, in our Starfinder game, it helped keep the general plot in your head between sessions.
